n Novi

One verse each day, on your Lock Screen.

Novi is a quiet iPhone app that places a single verse on your Lock Screen every day. No account, no ads, no noise.

Add Novi to your Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold your Lock Screen, then tap Customize.
  2. Choose Lock Screen, then tap the widget area under the clock.
  3. Find Novi and tap the rectangular widget to add it.
  4. Close the widget list and tap Done.

Common questions

The clock covers the verse.

On iOS 26 the clock can grow tall enough to cover a widget. Either shrink the clock (in Customize, drag the handle on its corner up as far as it goes) or drag the Novi widget to the bottom of the Lock Screen.

When does the verse change?

At midnight in your time zone. iOS decides exactly when widgets refresh, so the Lock Screen may update a few minutes after midnight. Opening Novi always shows the current verse.

How is “verses read” counted?

Every day since you installed Novi counts, because the verse is on your Lock Screen whether or not you open the app.

Where are my favorites stored?

Only on your iPhone. Novi has no account and no servers. Deleting the app deletes your favorites.

Which translation does Novi use?

The King James Version (KJV).
