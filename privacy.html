Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Novi shows one verse each day in the app and on your Lock Screen. It was built to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains exactly what that means.

The short version

Novi does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Novi does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

To work, Novi saves a few things on your iPhone only:

  • The verses you heart, and the day each one appeared.
  • A log of the verses Novi has shown you, used for Past verses and the “verses read” count.
  • The day you installed Novi, and whether you have finished the welcome screens.

This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. It is shared only between the Novi app and the Novi widget on the same iPhone. Deleting Novi deletes all of it.

Sharing a verse

When you tap Share, Novi hands the verse text to the standard iOS share sheet. Whatever app you choose to share with handles it under its own privacy policy. Novi does not see who you share with.

Children

Novi collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Novi adds features that need data, such as accounts or sharing with friends, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
